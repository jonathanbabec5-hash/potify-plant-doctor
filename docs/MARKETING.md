# Plant Doctor marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Home growers and horticulture-tool developers. **Offer.** Turn a grower's observations into a short list of causes and a sensible next check.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Publish a worked yellow-leaf example showing which observation changes the recommended next check. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Try one example and submit a reproducible correction. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 5 actionable fixture reviews. |

### Sales test

Show a three-page notebook sample to five volunteer reviewers. Proposed offer is a $6 digital care log, with two paid downloads as the first sales test after delivery is ready. Test interest in a paid illustrated care notebook after users finish a free assessment.

### Measurement and decision

Use `plant-doctor-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** Both rule libraries and the original website diagnosis code are recovered. Case records, private grow histories, photos, an independent accuracy study and parity with the standalone Replit project remain open. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
